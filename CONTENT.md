ExtraTools adds tools and machines that round out existing Slimefun mechanics: a Hammer that pulverizes blocks, a
Gold Transmuter, two Electric Composters, a Cobblestone Generator, a Vaporizer for salt and sulfate, a Concrete
Factory and a Pulverizer.

## :floppy_disk: Installation

| ExtraTools | Slimefun5 | Minecraft |
|---|---|---|
| UNOFFICIAL | 5.3.3+ | 1.8.x to 26.x |

Download the latest release and place it in your server's `plugins/` folder. Slimefun5 must be installed first.
